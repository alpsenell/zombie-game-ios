Privacy Policy

Last Stand: Deadzone · Effective 27 September 2026

The short version: there are no ads, no accounts and no tracking. The only data the game can send is anonymous gameplay statistics, and only if you tap Allow.

What the app stores on your device

Your progress, settings, run history and a record of which in-app purchases you own are stored locally on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Anonymous gameplay statistics (opt-in)

On first launch the game asks whether you want to help improve it. Nothing is sent unless you tap Allow. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Anonymous analytics. Turning it off stops sending, clears any unsent statistics and forgets the anonymous install id.

If you opt in, the game sends anonymous gameplay events to TelemetryDeck (TelemetryDeck GmbH, hosted in the EU), which processes them on our behalf. What is sent:

  • gameplay events, such as runs started and ended, waves reached, bosses, perks, weapons used, screens viewed and which product was purchased (by product id, never prices or payment details);
  • the app version and platform;
  • a hashed random install id, which is not linked to your Apple Account, Game Center or any other account;
  • a random session id.

No names, Game Center aliases or ids, advertising identifiers, location, contacts or free text are sent. The statistics are used only to understand how the game is played and to improve balance and stability. See TelemetryDeck's privacy policy.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the game submits scores to leaderboards, reports achievement progress, and uses Game Center matchmaking for online co-op. Scores carry a small code describing your character's outfit so other players can see it on the leaderboard. Co-op traffic goes peer-to-peer through Apple's GameKit; the developer runs no servers. Your Game Center data is handled by Apple under your Game Center settings.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. The game keeps a local note of what you own so it can unlock that content. No receipt or payment information is sent anywhere by the game.

Tracking

The game does not track you across apps or websites, does not use the advertising identifier, and does not share data with data brokers or advertisers.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version changes any of the above, this page and the App Store privacy label will be updated first.
