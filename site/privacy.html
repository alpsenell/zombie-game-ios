Privacy Policy

Horde Breakers · Effective 27 September 2026

The short version: the game collects no data. There are no ads, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking.

What the app stores on your device

Your progress, settings, run history and a record of which in-app purchases you own are stored locally on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

What the developer collects

Nothing. The game contains no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising code, and the developer runs no servers. No personal data, usage data or identifiers are sent to the developer or any third party.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the game submits scores to leaderboards, reports achievement progress, and uses Game Center matchmaking for online co-op. Scores carry a small code describing your character's outfit so other players can see it on the leaderboard. Co-op traffic goes peer-to-peer through Apple's GameKit; the developer runs no servers. Your Game Center data is handled by Apple under your Game Center settings.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. The game keeps a local note of what you own so it can unlock that content. No receipt or payment information is sent anywhere by the game.

Tracking

The game does not track you across apps or websites, does not use the advertising identifier, and does not share data with data brokers or advertisers.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version changes any of the above, this page and the App Store privacy label will be updated first.
