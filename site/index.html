Frequently asked

Is the game free? Yes. Every map, mode and scrap weapon can be earned by playing. Optional in-app purchases add four exclusive weapons, four cosmetic outfits and a Season Pass for each season.

I bought something on another device. Open the Armory and tap Restore. Weapons, outfits and Season Passes restore on any device signed in to the same Apple Account.

What does the Season Pass include? The premium reward track of that season only: scrap, card flair and that season's outfit. It gives no gameplay advantage, and rewards you reached before buying can be claimed afterwards.

Leaderboards, co-op or achievements aren't working. Sign in to Game Center in iOS Settings, then relaunch the game. Daily Challenge and Ranked can be played without an account; online co-op needs Game Center.

When do the daily and weekly boards reset? Daily at 00:00 UTC. Ranked Weekly on Mondays at 00:00 UTC.

Refunds. Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
